Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the Dono Bola mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Dono Bola stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Your chosen display name (optional)
  • Learning progress (stars, completed lessons)
  • Language preference (UZ/EN)

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. All data remains on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

The locally stored information is used solely to:

  • Track your learning progress
  • Display your personalized greeting
  • Remember your language preference

3. Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

4. Children's Privacy

While Dono Bola is an educational language learning app, it is intended for users aged 13 and older in accordance with Google Play policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device. You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app data in your device settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
